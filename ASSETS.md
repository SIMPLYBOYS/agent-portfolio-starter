# Asset licensing

The MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the source code in this repository only.

It does NOT cover anything under `public/assets/` (images, videos, posters,
screen recordings, screenshots). Those files keep their own sources and
terms, listed in `public/credits.html`. In particular:

- AI re-renders of Kobayashi Kiyochika prints are labeled "AI 重繪・非原作";
  the originals are public-domain works held by the National Diet Library.
- Pixel Office recordings and screenshots show LimeZu artwork and are for
  showcasing that project only.
- `public/assets/cogito-agent/LICENSE` carries that project's own MIT license.
